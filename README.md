Chek 2.0 is out, free for iPhone and iPad. It reads your own files, and
libraries on a Chek server you run, with Komga and Kavita read through it.
Manga and manhwa are at its heart, alongside e-books and every other kind of
comic. It's the successor to Chibi Manga, rebuilt from the ground up.

No ads, no tracking, and no account needed.
